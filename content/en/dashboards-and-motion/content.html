Claude Dashboards: ask quick questions of your company’s data

Connect your company’s data platform, such as Amazon Redshift, BigQuery, ClickHouse, Databricks, or Snowflake, and ask a question in plain language. Claude pulls answers from your data, builds the dashboard, and keeps it current as the data changes. It works with your other connectors too. For example, you can ask for a dashboard of your Salesforce opportunities. Dashboards work well for quick, exploratory questions, like how this week’s signups compare with last month’s. Click any number to see the query behind it, or ask Claude to explain it. Each chart shows when its data was last refreshed.

Dashboards work alongside your BI and analytics tools. When a question needs deeper analysis, send the dashboard from Claude straight to Amplitude, Grafana, Hex, Mixpanel, Omni, Perplexity, PostHog, or Sigma and pick up the analysis from there, with Looker, monday.com, and Tableau coming soon.

Claude Motion: explain your ideas with animation

Ask Claude to turn a quarterly report into a 30-second explainer for an all-hands, add motion to a chart in a board deck, or make a product walkthrough for customer onboarding. Claude builds an animation from your prompt, and it plays like a short video. You can adjust it in the editor or ask Claude to change it, then download it as an MP4 file.

Claude Motion is built for explaining your own work. Claude writes code that animates your text, charts, shapes, and images, so you can change any word, number, or timing. It doesn’t use a video generation model, so there’s no generated footage and no AI-generated people. When you want to take it further, open it in Adobe, Descript, HeyGen, Higgsfield, invideo, Luma AI, or Runway, with Canva and Captions coming soon.

Claude Design has moved into Claude

Claude Design started at its own URL, claude.ai/design. Since September 16, Design also works inside every Claude conversation, where it can use your context, files, and connectors. Running two versions slows down fixes for both, so we’re folding the standalone site into Claude. It stays at claude.ai/design (opens in new tab) until December 14, so you have time to move over.

  • Migrate your design systems today. On the Artifacts page in Claude, click "Migrate team design systems." One migration brings over every design system in your organization, and the originals stay unchanged in the standalone version until it closes.
  • Your projects need nothing from you yet. They keep working in the standalone version until it closes.
  • Save your chats and comments. Chats with Claude and comments on your projects stay in the standalone version and won't be available after it closes. Public links to standalone projects stop working then too.

Getting started

Open Claude and ask for what you need, like “Build a dashboard of this quarter’s revenue by region,” “Animate how our pricing plans compare for the sales kickoff,” or “Turn these notes into a deck for Monday.” Start with the dashboard you’d wait for, or the report that needs more than a slide.
