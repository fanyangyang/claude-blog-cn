We’ve added new agents and products to Claude Marketplace, where teams find partner tools and services to help them get more from Claude. The additions cover security, developer infrastructure, creative, and productivity.

Eligible enterprise customers can apply a portion of their committed Anthropic spend toward these solutions. This lets them use one commitment across Claude and partner products, whether they’re buying something new or expanding with a platform they already use.

There are now over 25 partner agents and products on the marketplace. Here are the latest additions and what teams can do with them.

Products and agents for securing code and systems

Chainguard: Chainguard Harness builds applications from source in an isolated microVM and probes the live app for exploitable vulnerabilities. It helps teams fix what Claude finds, with custom remediation recommendations for reproducible vulnerabilities.

Checkmarx: Checkmarx One pairs rules-based analysis with AI reasoning to find vulnerabilities in code. It helps teams prioritize findings and fix them before code ships.

Cyera: Cyera Agent Guardian inventories Claude deployments, governs permissions before Claude runs, and enforces policy on prompts, files, and tool calls. It helps teams test those controls through red teaming.

Rubrik: Rubrik Code Guardian uses frontier AI models from Anthropic to review a secure copy of GitHub or Azure DevOps repositories for multi-step attack paths. It helps security and engineering teams rank issues by severity and turn confirmed critical ones into tracked tickets.

Tanium: Tanium Cloud gives teams real-time visibility and control across their endpoints. It helps IT and security teams find and fix vulnerabilities, misconfigurations, and missing patches.

Development infrastructure for engineering teams

Datadog: Datadog Agent Observability traces agent execution through tools and services. It helps teams improve agent quality, performance, and cost efficiency by surfacing production patterns and recommending fixes.

Netlify: Netlify Enterprise deploys apps, sites, and agents to a global edge network, with preview URLs on every change. It helps teams take what they build with Claude from prototype to production with no infrastructure to configure, and includes SSO, role-based access, and audit logs.

Tools for marketing and creative work

AirOps: AirOps provides an agentic platform for AI search strategy, content, and multichannel execution. It helps marketing teams analyze brand performance in AI search and tie results to pipeline and revenue.

Runway: Runway generates and edits video, images, and audio. It helps creative and marketing teams turn a brief into finished assets and edit footage with natural language.

Webflow: Webflow provides built-in tools to build, manage, and optimize websites. It helps marketers, designers, developers, and agents make changes, with controls and governance built in.

Productivity apps for every team

Asana: Asana AI Teammates are prebuilt agents that use context from the Asana Work Graph, each with its own identity, scoped permissions, and audit trail. They help teams handle multi-step work, routing decisions that need judgment to the owner.

Glean: Glean connects company knowledge and workflows across systems. It helps employees find answers and complete multi-step tasks across connected apps.

monday: monday agents work inside projects, boards, docs, and meetings, using their owners and deadlines as context. They help PMO, IT, marketing, finance, and operations teams control what AI can access, approve, and spend.

Zoom: Zoom Agentic Search answers questions inside Claude using the latest meeting summaries, work conversations, documents, and connected third-party sources. Teams get permission-aware results without analyzing raw transcripts or chaining tools together.
